## Entry format

Copy this format below the Decisions heading when a real decision exists. Replace all field instructions with project-specific content.

### DEC-### — Decision title

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Scope: Local / System / Product, naming the affected area
Applies to: Release, platform, feature, mode, or audience boundary
Status: Proposed / Confirmed / Rejected / Superseded
Evidence status: Untested / Qualitative evidence / Quantitative evidence / Mixed / Inconclusive

**Problem and context:** The player situation and relevant established rules.

**Decision:** The selected or proposed behavior, with exact scope.

**Alternatives:** Meaningful options considered, when useful.

**Rationale:** Expected player experience and decisive tradeoff.

**Costs and risks:** Production and experience consequences.

**Validation:** Hypothesis, method, and revision signal; link actual findings separately.

**Confirmation:** Who or which authoritative record confirmed what, and when. Use “Pending” for a proposal.

**Context effect:** None, or the exact CTX IDs created, updated, or retired.

**Supersedes / superseded by:** Related IDs, or “None.”

**Status history:**

- YYYY-MM-DD: Initial status and its source.
