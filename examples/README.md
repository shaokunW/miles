# Examples: choose a mechanism, then inspect the boundary

Classic games are reference cases. The worked examples are original fictional projects. Neither category supplies confirmed facts about the developer's active game.

Each classic case separates a sourced description from a Miles interpretation, a possible transfer, and a test. The interpretation describes a hypothesis worth considering. It establishes no guaranteed outcome, historical intent beyond the source, or observed player study.

| Design question | Case | Important transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| How can play introduce a reusable idea? | [Super Mario Bros.](classics/super-mario-bros-1-1.md) | New players may need explicit control support. |
| How can guidance, recovery, and fiction work together? | [Portal](classics/portal-companion-cube.md) | Preserve the puzzle the encounter is meant to contain. |
| How can players choose their next destination? | [Breath of the Wild](classics/breath-of-the-wild.md) | Space, movement, and destination legibility must support choice. |
| How can an arc make companionship meaningful? | [Journey](classics/journey.md) | Emotional intentions and social outcomes vary. |
| Can clear information preserve hard decisions? | [Into the Breach](classics/into-the-breach.md) | Intentional hidden information may belong to another game's challenge. |
| What agency remains after automation? | [Factorio](classics/factorio.md) | Complexity tolerance and fantasy determine the right responsibility shift. |
