# Source registry

Registry date: **2026-09-20**.

V2 review: PKG-01, PKG-02, REF-05, and REF-08 were reopened; REF-09 and REF-10 were added and their stated pages inspected. Other entries and their access notes are carried from the supplied V1.1 release record. V2 does not claim fresh inspection of every inherited source or a new viewing of its recordings/PDFs.

This package contains original instructions, analyses, and examples. It links to reference material rather than bundling books, recordings, slides, screenshots, or game assets. Titles and names identify their respective works; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

“Inspected” means the stated material was accessible during preparation. A book overview, store description, or talk abstract has a narrower evidentiary scope than a full book, observed game session, or watched recording. External pages may change after the check date.

## Classic cases

### CASE-01

**Practical Game Design: The Rule of Threes.** Lars Doucet, March 4, 2010.  
URL: https://www.fortressofdoors.com/practical-game-design-the-rule-of-threes/  
Inspected: author's analysis of playable variation in Super Mario Bros.  
Evidence class: a game designer's original analysis, not a Nintendo production account or player study.  
Boundary: descriptions of learning are interpretive; a particular repetition count has no universal status. The Miles case avoids attributing Doucet's interpretation to Nintendo's stated intent.

### CASE-02

**Integrating Narrative and Design: A Portal Post-Mortem.** Kim Swift and Erik Wolpaw, GDC 2008.  
URL: https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2008/GDC2008_PortalPostMortem.pdf  
Publication index: https://www.valvesoftware.com/en/publications  
Inspected: developer slides; rendered cover and PDF pages 25, 29, 31, counted from 1.  
Evidence class: primary developer account.  
Use: Companion Cube encounter iteration, recovery cost, environmental hints, dialogue, and later mechanical preparation.  
Boundary: this case concerns the documented encounter; it makes no claim to reconstruct Portal's entire onboarding.

### CASE-03

**The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — official game page.** Nintendo.  
URL: https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-games/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Breath-of-the-Wild-1173609.html  
Inspected: official feature descriptions.  
Evidence class: publisher description.  
Use: searching for destinations, climbing, scouting, and traversal options.  
Boundary: the Miles interpretation of self-directed goals is analytical. The page supplies no measured motivation or retention result.

### CASE-04

**Journey — official game page.** thatgamecompany.  
URL: https://thatgamecompany.com/journey/  
Inspected: developer description of the adventure and companion encounters.  
Evidence class: primary developer description.  
Use: the distant mountain, journey structure, and the possibility of companionship.  
Boundary: feelings vary between players and sessions; the description supplies no guaranteed emotional response.

### CASE-05

**Into the Breach — official Steam product description.** Subset Games.
